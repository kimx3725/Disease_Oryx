## Data
The scimitar-horned oryx was classified as Extinct in the Wild by the IUCN Red List from 2000 to December 2023, when the species was downlisted to Endangered (IUCN SSC ASG, 2023). Due to the species’ extremely threatened status and history of hunting by humans, data supporting this research are sensitive and unavailable publicly. The oryx movement data are owned by the Environment Agency - Abu Dhabi (EAD). They are available to qualified researchers by contacting the Director of Environmental Information Management at EAD (https://www.ead.gov.ae/en) and requesting an EAD Data Usage Agreement to access GPS tracking data of re-introduced oryx in Chad.
